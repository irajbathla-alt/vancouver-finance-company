Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026

Vancouver Finance Company Inc. respects your privacy. This notice explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information submitted through our business-financing eligibility form.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, business name, phone number, email address, business industry, time in business, estimated monthly revenue, requested financing amount, estimated credit range, and other information you voluntarily provide.

How We Use Information

  • To assess potential business-financing options.
  • To contact you about your inquiry and request supporting information.
  • To refer or present an application to suitable financing providers with your authorization.
  • To maintain records, improve our services, prevent fraud, and meet legal or regulatory requirements.

Disclosure

Information may be shared with prospective lenders, financing providers, authorized service providers, professional advisers, or authorities when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Protection and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law.

Your Choices

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, or withdraw consent to future contact, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. Withdrawing consent may limit our ability to provide financing-related services.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, contact the Privacy Officer of Vancouver Finance Company Inc. using the contact information provided on our main website.
